What if colors disappeared?

Imagine a garden in shades of gray, then practice telling an object’s shape and color apart.

Watch on YouTube

In real life

The episode imagines color disappearing. In real life we see colors when light reaches our eyes, and different objects reflect different parts of that light.

Try it yourself

Draw two trees with the same shape. Color one green and leave the other in pencil. Name one thing that stayed the same and one that changed.
