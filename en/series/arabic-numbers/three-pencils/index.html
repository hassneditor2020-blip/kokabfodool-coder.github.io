Arabic number 3 with three pencils

Count three pencils with Lumaa and write ٣ when you finish.

Watch on YouTube
٣ثَلاثَة

What did we learn?

Point to a different pencil as you say each number. Three is the total number of pencils.

Try it yourself

Put three small objects on a table. Move them around and count them again.
