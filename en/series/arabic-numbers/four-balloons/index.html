Arabic number 4 with four balloons

Count four balloons with Mishaal and learn the Arabic numeral ٤.

What did we learn?

Point to each shape once. The last number you say tells you how many shapes are in the group.

Try it yourself

Draw four balloons in different colors. Write ٤ under the group.
