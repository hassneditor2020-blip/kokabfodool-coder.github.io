Which animal rests upside down?

Guess which animal grips with its feet and rests with its head pointing down.

Watch on YouTube

The clue

This animal flies. Many kinds rest hanging by their feet in sheltered places.

Try it yourself

Before revealing the answer, choose between an owl, a bat, and a fish. Explain which clue helped you.

Reveal the answer

A bat. Its claws grip the perch while its body hangs down.
