Which animal has three hearts?

Solve Fudool’s three-hearts riddle and learn about a sea animal with eight arms.

Watch on YouTube

The clue

This animal lives in the sea and has eight arms. It can hide among rocks and change its appearance.

Try it yourself

Draw the animal after you guess. Count its arms, then draw the three hearts next to it.

Reveal the answer

It’s an octopus. Two hearts send blood to the gills, and the third sends it to the rest of the body.
