About Kawkab Fudool

Kawkab Fudool is a learning project for children ages 3 to 10, combining short animated stories with play and discovery.

Meet the Kawkab Fudool characters

Lumaa teaches letters and numbers, Fudool sets riddles, ROBO explores experiments, and Mishaal discovers geography and nature. The What If? character begins each adventure with a question about our world.

Contact for adults

Parents and teachers can reach the project through the official Kawkab Fudool Instagram account.
